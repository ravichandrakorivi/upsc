**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Potholes and gorges are fluvial erosional features, cirques are glacial erosional features, and sea caves are coastal erosional features.

### Q80. Consider the following sequence:

**Erosion → transportation → reduction in transporting capacity → deposition**

Which principle of geomorphic evolution is best represented?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> Material removed from one location can become the basis for depositional landforms elsewhere<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Erosion and deposition are mutually exclusive processes<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Deposition occurs only in tectonically active regions<br>
<strong>D.</strong> Transportation always increases the carrying capacity of an agent

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Geomorphic agents can remove and transport material from one location and deposit it when their energy or carrying capacity declines, linking erosional and depositional landscapes.
