### Q4. Consider the following statements regarding relative humidity:

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Moisture is lost on the windward side through condensation and rainfall. Descending air on the leeward side warms, increasing its moisture-holding capacity and producing comparatively dry conditions.
