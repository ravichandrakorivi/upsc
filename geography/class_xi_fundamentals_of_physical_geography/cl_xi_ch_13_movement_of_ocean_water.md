### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding movements of ocean water:

1. Ocean currents involve the continuous movement of large volumes of water in a definite direction.
2. Waves primarily represent the transfer of energy rather than the net transport of water over long distances.
3. Tides are primarily horizontal movements of ocean water caused by winds.
4. Upwelling of subsurface water is a form of vertical movement of ocean water.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4
