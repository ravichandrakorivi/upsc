### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding atmospheric pressure:

1. Atmospheric pressure is the weight of a column of air exerted on a unit area.
2. At sea level, atmospheric pressure is generally higher than at greater elevations.
3. The decrease of pressure with height occurs at a uniform rate throughout the atmosphere.
4. Horizontal variations in pressure are important in generating winds.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Atmospheric pressure is the weight of the air column per unit area. It generally decreases with altitude because the overlying air column becomes smaller. The rate of decrease is not uniform. Horizontal pressure differences generate pressure-gradient forces and hence wind.
