### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the atmosphere:

1. The atmosphere is a mixture of gases, water vapour and dust particles.

3. Atmospheric density is greatest near the Earth's surface.
4. The atmosphere completely ends at a sharply defined upper boundary.

<div>
