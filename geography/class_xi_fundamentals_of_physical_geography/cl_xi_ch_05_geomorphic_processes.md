### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the unevenness of the Earth's surface:

### Q80. Consider the following integrated sequence:

**Climate → weathering → regolith → biological colonisation → humus accumulation → mature soil**

Which of the following conclusions is most appropriate?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> Soil formation is controlled by a single physical process<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Soil development involves interaction between climatic, physical and biological processes over time<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Biological activity begins only after mature soil has already formed<br>
<strong>D.</strong> Weathering has no role in soil development

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** Soil development begins with weathered or transported material and progressively involves microorganisms, plants, animals, organic matter accumulation, porosity and water retention. The process operates through interacting factors over time.
