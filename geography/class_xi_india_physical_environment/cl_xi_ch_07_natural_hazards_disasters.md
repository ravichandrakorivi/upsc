### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding natural hazards and disasters:

1. A natural hazard represents a potential source of harm.
2. Every natural hazard necessarily develops into a disaster.
3. The magnitude of destruction and damage is relevant in determining whether an event becomes a disaster.
4. The socio-environmental characteristics of an affected area can influence the consequences of a hazard.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** A hazard has the potential to cause harm, whereas a disaster involves sufficiently large-scale destruction and disruption. Not every hazard becomes a disaster. Local socio-environmental conditions and human vulnerability influence the consequences.
