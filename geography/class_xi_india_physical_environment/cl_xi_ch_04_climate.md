### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding weather and climate:

1. Weather represents the momentary state of the atmosphere.
2. Climate refers to the average of weather conditions over a longer period.
3. Weather generally changes more slowly than climate.
4. Climatic change can be identified only through observations spanning several centuries.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 3 and 4 only

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Weather is the short-term or momentary atmospheric condition, whereas climate represents the average pattern over a longer period. Weather can change within a day or week, while climatic changes are generally much slower.
