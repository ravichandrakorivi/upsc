### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the geographical extent of India:

1. India's north-south extent is greater than its east-west extent in terms of actual ground distance.
2. India's latitudinal and longitudinal extents are both roughly 30 degrees.
3. The east-west ground distance is smaller partly because the distance between meridians decreases towards the poles.
4. The distance between two parallels of latitude also decreases towards the poles.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** India's north-south distance is about 3,214 km, compared with about 2,933 km east-west. Although both angular extents are roughly 30°, meridians converge towards the poles, making the actual east-west distance smaller. The distance between parallels remains essentially constant.

### Q2. If two places in India differ by approximately 30° of longitude, the difference in their local solar time would be approximately:

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 30 minutes<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 hour<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2 hours<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 4 hours

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **C**

> **Explanation:** Earth rotates 360° in approximately 24 hours, or 15° per hour. Therefore, a 30° longitudinal difference corresponds to about 2 hours.
