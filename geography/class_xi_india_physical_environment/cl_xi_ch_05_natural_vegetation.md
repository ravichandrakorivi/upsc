### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding natural vegetation:

1. Natural vegetation develops without deliberate human cultivation.
2. A plant community may be considered natural vegetation when it has remained undisturbed long enough for species to adjust to local climatic and soil conditions.
3. The presence of a species in a forest automatically makes all occurrences of that species natural vegetation.
4. Climate and soil are important determinants of the spatial variation of natural vegetation in India.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Natural vegetation refers to plant communities left undisturbed over a long period, allowing species to adjust to climate and soil. A planted occurrence of a species does not become natural vegetation merely because the same species occurs naturally elsewhere.
