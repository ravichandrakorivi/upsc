### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the geological divisions of India:

1. The Peninsular Block represents an ancient and relatively stable geological unit.
2. The Himalayas and other Peninsular mountains are younger and more tectonically active than the Peninsular Block.
3. The Indo-Ganga-Brahmaputra Plain developed primarily through prolonged accumulation of sediments.
4. Geological divisions and physiographic divisions of India are completely unrelated to each other.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>

### Q2. Which one of the following best explains the contrast between the Peninsular Block and the Himalayan mountains?
