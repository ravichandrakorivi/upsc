# Class VII : 9. Life in Deserts

## UPSC Prelims Practice Questions

### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding desert environments:

1. Low rainfall is an important characteristic of deserts.
2. Deserts may experience either extremely high or extremely low temperatures.
3. Sparse vegetation is associated with limited availability of water.
4. All deserts necessarily have extensive sand-covered surfaces.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Deserts are characterised by low rainfall, scanty vegetation and extreme temperatures, which may be either very high or very low. A desert need not consist entirely of sand; the Sahara also contains gravel plains, elevated plateaus and bare rocky surfaces.

### Q2. Consider the following statements regarding the climate of the Sahara:

1. Strong heating of bare surfaces during the day contributes to very high daytime temperatures.
2. The absence of substantial cloud cover favours rapid loss of heat after sunset.
3. The Sahara therefore exhibits a relatively large difference between daytime and night-time temperatures.
4. The high daytime temperature prevents the Sahara from experiencing near-freezing night temperatures.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** Sand and bare rocks become intensely heated during the day and radiate heat. The clear, cloudless conditions allow substantial heat loss after sunset, so nights can become very cold, with temperatures nearing 0°C. Thus, the fourth statement is incorrect.

### Q3. Assertion (A): An oasis can support a settled population in an otherwise arid desert environment.

### Reason (R): An oasis develops where underground water reaches the surface, providing water and relatively fertile conditions for cultivation.

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.<br>
<strong>C.</strong> A is true, but R is false.<br>
<strong>D.</strong> A is false, but R is true.

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Wind can create depressions by removing sand. Where underground water reaches the surface in such depressions, an oasis forms. These areas are fertile and can support settlement and cultivation.

### Q5. Consider the following statements regarding the aridity of Ladakh:

1. Its high altitude contributes to extremely low temperatures.
2. Its location in the rain shadow of the Himalayas contributes to low rainfall.
3. Low rainfall and high aridity together contribute to sparse vegetation.
4. Its cold climate implies that the region receives abundant snowfall throughout the year.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** Ladakh's high altitude contributes to its extremely cold climate, while its location in the rain shadow of the Himalayas results in very little rainfall. High aridity consequently produces sparse vegetation. The fourth statement is incorrect; a cold desert cannot be assumed to receive abundant snowfall throughout the year.

### Q6. Consider the following statements regarding human adaptation in desert regions:

1. Nomadic pastoralism can be practised where settled agriculture is constrained by limited water availability.
2. Availability of water at oases permits cultivation and more permanent settlement.
3. Livestock can provide food and raw materials such as milk, hides and hair.
4. Desert environments necessarily prevent all forms of economic diversification.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** In the Sahara, groups such as the Bedouins and Tuaregs practise nomadic livestock rearing. Oases support settled cultivation. Livestock provide milk, hides and hair, which can be used for food and various products. Desert regions can also develop activities such as mining, tourism and trade, making the fourth statement incorrect.

### Q7. Consider the following statements regarding economic transformation in the Sahara:

1. Discovery of oil has contributed to changes in economic activities and settlement patterns.
2. Development of roads can reduce the importance of traditional camel-based transport in some activities.
3. Expansion of oil and gas operations can encourage some nomadic herders to adopt urban livelihoods.
4. Resource development necessarily preserves traditional occupations without altering the cultural landscape.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Oil discovery in Algeria, Libya and Egypt has transformed parts of the Sahara. Superhighways and trucks have reduced the relative importance of traditional camel routes in activities such as salt trade. Some nomadic herders have moved towards city-based employment in oil and gas operations.
