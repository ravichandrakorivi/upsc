# Class VII : 9. Life in Temperate Grasslands

## UPSC Prelims Practice Questions

### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the Prairies:

1. Their continental location contributes to considerable seasonal temperature variation.
2. Moderate rainfall supports the growth of grass.
3. The absence of a north-south barrier permits the local Chinook wind to blow across the region.
4. The Chinook is a cold winter wind that prolongs the period of snow cover.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** The Prairies lie in the heart of a continent and experience a continental climate with extreme temperatures. Moderate rainfall is suitable for grass. The absence of a north-south barrier permits the Chinook to blow. Chinook is a hot winter wind that raises temperature and melts snow, rather than prolonging snow cover.

### Q2. Consider the following statements regarding agricultural and pastoral activities in the Prairies:

1. Areas receiving more than about 50 cm of rainfall are suitable for farming because the soil is fertile.
2. Where rainfall is very little or unreliable, grasses tend to be short and sparse.
3. The latter conditions favour cattle rearing rather than intensive crop cultivation.
4. The same rainfall conditions necessarily produce identical land-use patterns throughout the Prairies.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Areas receiving more than about 50 cm of rainfall are considered suitable for farming, while areas with little or unreliable rainfall have shorter, sparser grass and are suitable for cattle rearing. The fourth statement is an unwarranted generalisation.

3. Winter rainfall scarcity can produce drought conditions.
4. Their mild climate is partly influenced by the Indian Ocean.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4<br>

<div>

<strong>B.</strong> Different parts of the region provide conditions for crop cultivation and livestock rearing, while rich mineral deposits support mining and related industries.<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Mining has completely replaced agriculture and livestock rearing in the Velds.<br>
<strong>D.</strong> The region's economy is based exclusively on export-oriented sheep farming.

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** The Velds have areas where crops can be cultivated, extensive sheep and cattle rearing, and rich deposits of coal, iron, gold and diamonds. These resources have supported mining and industrial development alongside agriculture and livestock activities.

### Q8. Consider the following statements regarding industrial development in the Prairies and Velds:

1. Availability of mineral resources can support industrial development.
2. Transport networks can facilitate the movement of agricultural and mineral products.
3. Food-processing industries can develop where agriculture and dairy farming generate substantial output.
4. Industrialisation in a grassland region necessarily implies the disappearance of all agricultural activities.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Mineral deposits, transport networks and agricultural output can all contribute to industrial development. In the Prairies, extensive agriculture and dairy farming support food-processing industries, while coal and iron support industrialisation. Industrial development does not necessarily eliminate agriculture.

### Q9. Consider the following statements regarding the drainage of the Prairies:

1. The American Prairies are drained by tributaries of the Mississippi.
2. The Canadian Prairies are drained by tributaries of the Saskatchewan River.
3. The Prairie grasslands form a single drainage basin irrespective of political boundaries.
4. The drainage pattern reflects the transboundary geographical extent of the grassland region.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

### Q10. Consider the following statements regarding vegetation variation within the Prairies:

1. The Prairies are predominantly treeless.
2. Trees such as willows, alders and poplars can occur where water is available.
3. Areas with very little or unreliable rainfall tend to have shorter and sparser grass.
4. The presence of trees is therefore unrelated to local availability of water.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** The Prairies are largely treeless, but trees can grow in locations where water is available, particularly near river valleys. Lower or unreliable rainfall is associated with shorter and sparser grass. Thus, local water availability does influence vegetation distribution.

### Q13. Consider the following statements regarding the seasonal contrast between the Prairies and the Velds:

1. The Prairies lie in the Northern Hemisphere, whereas the Velds lie in the Southern Hemisphere.
2. When the Prairies experience winter, the Velds generally experience summer.
3. July is the coldest month in the Velds, whereas the Prairies experience winter conditions around the same time.
4. The seasonal cycle is reversed between the two regions because they lie in opposite hemispheres.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **C**

> **Explanation:** The Prairies are in the Northern Hemisphere and the Velds in the Southern Hemisphere. Consequently, their seasons are broadly opposite. July is the coldest month in the Velds, corresponding to summer in the Northern Hemisphere, while the Prairies experience winter around this period.
