### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the interior of the Earth:

1. The Earth is composed of several concentric layers.
2. The crust is the outermost and thinnest major layer.
3. The mantle lies immediately beneath the crust.
4. The core constitutes the largest volume of the Earth.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>
