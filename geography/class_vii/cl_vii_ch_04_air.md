3. Snow, sleet and hail are other forms of precipitation.
4. All precipitation reaches the ground exclusively in liquid form.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

### Q47. Which one of the following correctly identifies the three major types of rainfall based on mechanism?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> Convectional, orographic and cyclonic rainfall<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Convectional, frontal and monsoonal rainfall<br>

### Q50. Consider the following statements:

1. A rise in temperature can increase the capacity of air to hold water vapour.
2. Rising moist air can cool and undergo condensation.
3. Condensation contributes to cloud formation.
4. Precipitation occurs when condensed water particles become sufficiently heavy to fall.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **D**

> **Explanation:** Warmer air can hold more water vapour. As moist air rises and cools, condensation produces water droplets that form clouds; when these particles become sufficiently heavy, precipitation occurs.
