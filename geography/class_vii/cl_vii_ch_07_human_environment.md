# Class VII : 7. Human Environment - Settlement, Transport and Communication

## UPSC Prelims Practice Questions

### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding human settlements:

1. The availability of water and fertile land can influence the location of settlements.
2. The development of agriculture encouraged the establishment of permanent settlements.
3. The growth of trade, commerce and manufacturing can contribute to the enlargement of settlements.
4. Settlements in all regions necessarily become permanent once agriculture is introduced.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** Availability of water and fertile land favours settlement. Agriculture created the need for permanent homes, while trade, commerce and manufacturing contributed to the growth of settlements. However, it cannot be concluded that every settlement necessarily becomes permanent.

### Q2. Consider the following statements regarding rural settlement patterns:

1. Compact settlements consist of closely built dwellings.
2. Scattered settlements generally have dwellings spread over an extensive area.
3. Scattered settlements are commonly associated with hilly tracts, thick forests and regions of extreme climate.
4. Compact settlements necessarily occur only in regions of high population density.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** Compact settlements have closely built dwellings, while scattered settlements have dwellings distributed over a wider area. Scattered settlements are commonly found in hilly areas, thick forests and regions with extreme climatic conditions. The fourth statement is an unwarranted generalisation.

### Q3. A settlement is located in an area receiving heavy rainfall, where water tends to accumulate during the rainy season. Which of the following adaptations would be most consistent with the environmental conditions?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> Houses with slanting roofs and, where necessary, raised platforms or stilts<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Houses constructed entirely below ground level<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Houses with flat roofs designed to retain accumulated rainwater<br>
<strong>D.</strong> Houses built only from imported construction materials

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Slanting roofs facilitate the removal of rainwater, while raised platforms or stilts help keep houses above accumulated water.

### Q4. Consider the following pairs:

| Mode/Type | Characteristic |
|---|---|
| 1. Roadways | Particularly useful for short-distance transport |
| 2. Railways | Suitable for carrying heavy goods over long distances |
| 3. Waterways | Economical for heavy and bulky goods over long distances |
| 4. Airways | Most suitable where speed is less important than low cost |

Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 4 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Roads are commonly used, especially for shorter distances; railways efficiently carry people and heavy goods over long distances; and waterways are economical for heavy and bulky goods. Airways are fast but comparatively expensive, so the fourth pair is incorrect.

### Q5. Assertion (A): The railway network is generally more developed in plains than in difficult mountain terrain.

### Reason (R): Laying railway lines in difficult mountain terrain requires greater technological effort, and such railway lines are consequently fewer.

Select the correct answer using the code given below:

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.<br>
<strong>C.</strong> A is true, but R is false.<br>
<strong>D.</strong> A is false, but R is true.

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Railway networks are well developed over plains. Difficult mountain terrain presents greater engineering challenges, although technological advances have made construction possible in some such areas.

1. Inland waterways can use navigable rivers and lakes.
2. Sea routes are important for transporting goods between countries.
3. Airways are generally more expensive than other major modes of transport because of high fuel costs.
4. Airways are particularly useful for reaching remote areas where roads and railways are absent.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4<br>

### Q7. Consider the following statements regarding communication:

1. Newspapers, radio and television can communicate information to large numbers of people and are therefore forms of mass media.
2. Satellites have applications beyond communication, including weather forecasting and disaster warning.
3. Internet-based communication has increased the ability of people to interact across geographical distances.
4. The development of faster communication has reduced the interconnectivity of people and institutions.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** Mass media reaches large numbers of people, while satellites support activities such as weather forecasting and disaster warning. Internet-based communication has increased global interaction. The fourth statement is contrary to this trend.

### Q8. Which one of the following combinations best illustrates how physical conditions, economic activity and technology can jointly influence human geography?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> River valleys — availability of water and fertile land — growth of settlements<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Mountain terrain — easier railway construction — denser railway networks than plains<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Remote areas without roads — reduced usefulness of air transport — greater dependence on waterways in all cases<br>
<strong>D.</strong> Development of communication technology — reduced interaction between distant societies — decline of global connectivity

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** River valleys offer water and fertile land, encouraging settlement. Difficult mountain terrain generally makes railway construction more challenging. Airways can be particularly valuable in remote areas lacking road and rail connectivity, while communication technology has increased rather than reduced global connectivity.
