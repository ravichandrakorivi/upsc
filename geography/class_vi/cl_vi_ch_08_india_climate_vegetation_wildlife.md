### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding weather and climate:

1. Weather refers to day-to-day changes in atmospheric conditions.
2. Temperature, rainfall and sunshine are elements associated with weather.
3. Climate refers to average weather conditions measured over many years.
4. Weather and climate refer to exactly the same time scale.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br> <strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br> <strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br> <strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

**Answer**: **B**

**Explanation**: Weather concerns short-term, day-to-day atmospheric conditions, whereas climate refers to the average pattern of weather over a long period. Therefore, the time scales are different.
