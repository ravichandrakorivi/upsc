### Q1. Which one of the following best describes a globe?

## Heat Zones

### Q18. Which one of the following is the correct basis for identifying the Torrid Zone?

<div>
<strong>A.</strong> It lies between the Arctic Circle and North Pole<br>
<strong>B.</strong> It lies between the Tropic of Cancer and Tropic of Capricorn<br>
<strong>C.</strong> It lies between the two polar circles<br>
<strong>D.</strong> It lies between the Prime Meridian and 180° Meridian
</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** The Torrid Zone lies between the Tropic of Cancer and Tropic of Capricorn. The midday Sun can be directly overhead at least once a year at latitudes within this region. 

---

<strong>D.</strong> Frigid Zone
</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** The Torrid Zone receives the maximum heat because the midday Sun is overhead at least once a year over latitudes between the Tropic of Cancer and Tropic of Capricorn.

## Longitudes

### Q29. Why is latitude alone insufficient to precisely locate a place on Earth?

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** Different places can lie on the same parallel of latitude. Longitude is therefore required to determine the precise position of a place.

### Q49. Consider two places located at 15° E and 15° W respectively. What is the difference in their local times?
