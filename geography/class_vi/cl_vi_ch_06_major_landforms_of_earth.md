### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the formation of landforms:

1. Internal processes may cause upliftment or sinking of portions of the Earth's surface.
2. External processes include erosion and deposition.
3. Erosion tends to wear down the land surface, whereas deposition can rebuild it.
4. Landforms are shaped only by internal processes.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
