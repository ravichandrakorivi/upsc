### Q1. With reference to the major domains of the Earth, consider the following statements:

1. The Lithosphere represents the solid and rigid outer portion of the Earth.

2. The Hydrosphere includes water in solid, liquid and gaseous forms.

3. The Atmosphere is the narrow zone where land, water and air interact and where life exists.

4. The Biosphere encompasses the parts of the Earth where living organisms are found.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br> <strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br> <strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br> <strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

### Q4. Consider the following statements regarding elevation:

2. Mean sea level is conventionally taken as the reference level for measuring elevation.

3. The actual level of the sea surface remains exactly the same everywhere.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 only<br> <strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br> <strong>C.</strong> 2 and 3 only<br> <strong>D.</strong> 1, 2 and 3

</div>

**Answer:** **B**

> **Explanation:** Elevation refers to the height of a point relative to a reference level, and mean sea level (MSL) is commonly used as that reference. ### Q5. Consider the following statements:

1. Mount Everest rises nearly 9 km above mean sea level.

2. The deepest known point of the Mariana Trench lies more than 10 km below sea level.

3. The vertical difference between the summit of Mount Everest and the deepest known point of the Mariana Trench is greater than 18 km.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br> <strong>B.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br> <strong>C.</strong> 2 and 3 only<br> <strong>D.</strong> 1, 2 and 3

</div>

**Answer:** **D. 1, 2 and 3**

> **Explanation:**
>
> * Mount Everest has an elevation of approximately **8.85 km above mean sea level**.
>
> * The **Challenger Deep** in the Mariana Trench is approximately **10.9 km below sea level**.
>
>* Therefore, the approximate vertical difference between the two extremes is: **8.85 km + 10.9 km ≈ 19.75 km**. Hence, the vertical difference is **greater than 18 km**.
