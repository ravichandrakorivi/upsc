### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the Earth's rotation:

1. Rotation refers to the movement of the Earth around its own axis.

2. The rotation of the Earth is primarily responsible for the occurrence of day and night.

3. The period of rotation of the Earth is approximately 24 hours.

4. The circle of illumination coincides with the Earth's axis.
