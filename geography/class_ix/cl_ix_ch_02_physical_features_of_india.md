### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the major physiographic divisions of India:

1. The Himalayas and Northern Plains are geologically younger than the Peninsular Plateau.
2. The Peninsular Plateau is composed predominantly of old crystalline rocks.
3. The Himalayas represent a comparatively stable geological block.
4. The Northern Plains are largely formed by alluvial deposition.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** The Himalayas and Northern Plains are relatively young landforms, while the Peninsular Plateau is an ancient and comparatively stable landmass composed largely of crystalline rocks. The Himalayas are geologically unstable rather than stable.
