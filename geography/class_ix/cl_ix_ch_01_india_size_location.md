### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding the geographical extent of India:

1. The mainland of India lies entirely in the Northern Hemisphere.
2. The latitudinal extent of the mainland is greater than its longitudinal extent.
3. The longitudinal extent of India has implications for differences in local time across the country.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 3 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2 and 3

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **D**

> **Explanation:** India’s mainland lies entirely in the Northern Hemisphere. Its latitudinal and longitudinal extents are each about 30°, but the north-south extent is geographically greater than the east-west extent. The longitudinal spread produces a significant difference in local time between the eastern and western parts of the country.
