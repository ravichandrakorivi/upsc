### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding a drainage basin:

1. It is the area drained by a single river system.
2. Tributaries of the main river are included within its drainage basin.
3. A water divide separates adjacent drainage basins.
4. A drainage basin is necessarily confined to a single political boundary.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** A drainage basin includes the area drained by a main river and its tributaries. Elevated terrain such as mountains or uplands can form water divides between adjoining basins. A drainage basin can extend across political boundaries.
