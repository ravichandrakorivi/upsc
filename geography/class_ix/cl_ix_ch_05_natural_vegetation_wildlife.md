### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding natural vegetation:

1. Natural vegetation refers to plant communities that have grown without human aid.
2. Vegetation that has remained undisturbed by humans for a long period can be described as virgin vegetation.
3. Cultivated crops and orchards are included within natural vegetation if they are composed of indigenous species.
4. Indigenous species are those that are purely native to the region.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 3 and 4 only

</div>

<br>
