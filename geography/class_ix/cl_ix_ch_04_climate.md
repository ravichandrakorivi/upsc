### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding weather and climate:

1. Weather refers to the atmospheric condition over a place at a particular point of time.
2. Climate refers to the sum total of weather conditions and variations over a large area over a long period.
3. Weather and climate involve different basic atmospheric elements.
4. Short-term fluctuations in atmospheric conditions are primarily a characteristic of weather.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 3 and 4 only

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **A**

> **Explanation:** Weather describes the atmospheric state at a particular time, whereas climate describes long-term patterns and variations. Both involve the same basic elements such as temperature, pressure, wind, humidity and precipitation. Short-term fluctuations are characteristic of weather.
