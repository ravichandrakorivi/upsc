### Q42. Which of the following best captures the logic of Ricardian equivalence?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> Government borrowing permanently eliminates future taxation.<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Consumers ignore future government liabilities when making spending decisions.<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Government borrowing today may induce additional private saving in anticipation of future taxes.<br>
<strong>D.</strong> Taxation and borrowing always have opposite effects on national saving.

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **C**

> **Explanation:** The central idea is that forward-looking consumers recognise that borrowing today may imply higher taxes in the future and therefore increase saving today.

### Q50. Consider the following statements about the Government Budget:

1. Allocation addresses the provision of goods and services that markets may fail to provide adequately.
2. Redistribution operates through taxes and transfers affecting household disposable income.
3. Stabilisation involves using fiscal instruments to moderate fluctuations in output, employment and prices.
4. Fiscal deficit by itself establishes that government expenditure is being used entirely for productive investment.
5. Public debt should be assessed partly in relation to its effect on future economic growth.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1, 2 and 3 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 5 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3, 4 and 5 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** The three core budgetary functions are allocation, redistribution and stabilisation.
