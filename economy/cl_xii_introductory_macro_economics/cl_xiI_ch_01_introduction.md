### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding macroeconomics:

1. It primarily studies economic phenomena at the level of the economy as a whole.
2. Aggregate output, general price levels and employment are among its important concerns.
3. It necessarily treats every individual good and service as analytically distinct.
4. It examines interlinkages among different sectors of an economy.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 1, 2, 3 and 4

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

### Q2. Which one of the following best captures the distinction between microeconomics and macroeconomics?

### Q3. Consider the following statements about the representative good used in macroeconomic analysis:
