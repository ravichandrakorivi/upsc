### Q1. Consider the following statements regarding ex ante and ex post variables:

1. Ex ante values represent planned or intended values.
2. Ex post values represent values that have actually occurred.
3. Ex ante investment and ex post investment must always be equal.
4. The distinction between ex ante and ex post values is important in analysing macroeconomic equilibrium.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> 1 and 2 only<br>
<strong>B.</strong> 1, 2 and 4 only<br>
<strong>C.</strong> 1, 3 and 4 only<br>
<strong>D.</strong> 2, 3 and 4 only

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **B**

> **Explanation:** Ex ante refers to planned values, while ex post refers to actual outcomes. Planned and actual investment can differ because of unintended inventory changes. The distinction is therefore important for analysing equilibrium.

### Q2. A producer plans to increase inventories by ₹100 crore during a year. Due to unexpectedly high sales, inventories actually increase by only ₹70 crore. Which of the following is correct?

<div>

<strong>A.</strong> Ex ante investment is ₹70 crore and ex post investment is ₹100 crore.<br>
<strong>B.</strong> Both ex ante and ex post investment are ₹70 crore.<br>
<strong>C.</strong> Ex ante investment is ₹100 crore and ex post investment is ₹70 crore.<br>
<strong>D.</strong> The difference cannot be classified as inventory investment.

</div>

<br>

**Answer**: **C**

> **Explanation:** The planned addition to inventory is ex ante investment. The actual increase after accounting for unexpectedly higher sales is ex post investment. Hence they are ₹100 crore and ₹70 crore respectively.
